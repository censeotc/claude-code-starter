  • What we learned mapping a year’s worth of AI-enabled cyber threatsMAJOR

    Why it matters
    Anthropic mapped a year of AI-enabled cyber threats and published the LLM ATT&CK Navigator, providing PMs and security teams with concrete threat patterns and attack vectors. This establishes Anthropic as a thought leader in AI security and informs product roadmap decisions around model safety and capability limitations.
    Overview
    On June 3, 2026, Anthropic published research documenting AI-enabled cyber threats mapped to the MITRE ATT&CK framework. The work analyzed a full year of threat data and introduced the LLM ATT&CK Navigator, a tool for visualizing and understanding how LLMs are being exploited in real-world attacks. The research identifies specific attack patterns, including prompt injection, model extraction, and privilege escalation techniques enabled by LLM capabilities. This represents the first systematic taxonomy of AI-specific threats and serves as a baseline for measuring LLM impact on exploit development, particularly N-day vulnerabilities. The publication positions Anthropic's safety research as foundational to industry security standards.
