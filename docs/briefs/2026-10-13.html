  • Redesigning source control at AnthropicMINOR
    Oct 13
    Why it matters
    Anthropic's internal tooling evolution reflects scaling challenges that impact development velocity and team productivity. PMs should understand this signals how infrastructure decisions enable or constrain the pace of feature delivery and model development.
    Overview
    Anthropic identified source control as its primary development bottleneck after merge volume increased more than tenfold, prompting a five-month migration to a custom in-house version control system. The engineering post details the redesign rationale, migration methodology, and technical decisions made during the transition. The company moved away from a standard off-the-shelf system to purpose-built tooling optimized for their specific workflow patterns and scale. Key specifics on merge patterns, performance improvements, and rollout strategy are covered. This represents infrastructure modernization driven by operational scaling rather than a product or capability announcement. The post positions this as a behind-the-scenes engineering challenge solved through internal tool development rather than external partnership.
