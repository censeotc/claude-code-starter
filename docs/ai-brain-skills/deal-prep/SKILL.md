## What this skill does

Builds the Challenger Sale package for one specific deal: teach them something about their business, tailor it to the person in the room, and take control of next steps.

## Connections used

- **CRM (Airtable) + email + vault** — everything already known: prior contact, stage, who we're talking to, what's been said.
- **Research** — the prospect's website, reviews, visible marketing; their local competitors.
- **The teaching artifact** — the strongest opener is revenue-leakage math on THEIR numbers: dead estimates, slow follow-up, and idle customer lists priced out from whatever is visible (reviews volume, truck count, average ticket for their trade). Where Ahrefs is available, an `/aeo-snapshot` ("AI recommends your competitor, not you") is the alternate door-opener.
- **Know the buyer** — Owner/President buys found money and no rip-and-replace; GM/COO buys adoption and scoreboards without new software; Ops/Service Manager buys less chasing; Sales Manager buys a replacement pipeline. Frame the same insight for whoever is in the room.
- **Meetings (Granola)** — transcripts of any prior calls with them: their words, their objections.

## Output format

```
# Deal prep: <Company> — <call date>

**Who's in the room:** name, role, what they personally care about
**Where the deal stands:** stage, history, what they said last (their words)

## The insight (teach)
The one thing about THEIR business they don't know or underweight —
specific, evidenced, uncomfortable. Not our pitch; their problem.

## The reframe
From how they currently see it → to the frame where our offer is the
obvious answer. One paragraph, conversational, ready to say out loud.

## Tailoring
How this lands for this specific person/vertical — the numbers that
matter to an owner vs. a PE operator differ.

## Pushback plan
Top 3 likely objections, each with a constructive-tension response —
hold the frame, don't retreat to discounting.

## Take control
The specific next step to propose, with a date, and the fallback if
they stall.
```

## Rules

- The insight must survive the "so what" test: if they could say "we know, and it's fine," it's not an insight yet — dig further.
- Ground every claim about the prospect in something checkable (their site, reviews, data pulled this session). A wrong "insight" ends the deal.
- Rehearse mode: on request, role-play the prospect raising the pushback list so the operator can practice the responses.
